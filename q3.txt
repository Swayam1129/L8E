Q1. Which data field of your Sprite is the best candidate to move out into a separate
component type in an ECS setup? Choose the one that is most likely to apply to things
other than sprites. Briefly justify your choice.

The transform (mTransform, which holds the position and scale) is the best one to move
out. Pretty much every game object needs a position and size, not just sprites (enemies,
bullets, hitboxes, the camera), and systems like physics or collision only care about
where something is, not what it looks like.


Q2. What does this mean we have to do if we want to render multiple, possibly translucent
sprites on top of each other (think of windows with different colour tints stacked on top
of each other)?

Since the blend result depends on which colour is on top, we have to draw the sprites in
order from back to front, sorting them by depth so the farthest ones are drawn first and
the closest ones last. That way, each see-through sprite gets blended over whatever is
already behind it, just like looking through a stack of tinted windows.
